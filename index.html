Who I Am

> Vaibhav Sharma // CSE Student & Robotics Explorer

I am a B.Tech CSE-Robotics and AI student fascinated by the intersection where digital algorithms meet physical mechanics. While much of computer science happens purely inside the browser or the cloud, my true passion lies in giving code eyes, ears, and wheels.

I actively explore AI/ML, Computer Vision, Drones and Robotics. Whether it is engineering a serialized OpenCV vision pipeline for an autonomous ground vehicle or writing bare-metal firmware for sensor-actuated sorting rovers, I am driven by building practical, tangible technology.

I believe true engineering growth comes not from claiming unearned mastery, but from relentless curiosity, hands-on experimentation, and turning theoretical knowledge into functioning hardware and software prototypes.
